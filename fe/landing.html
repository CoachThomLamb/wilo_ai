How it works

Train. Track. Progress.
Repeat.

Most apps log what you did. Wilo closes the loop: what you did shapes what you do next.

  1. TrainToday's workout is on your phone. Do the work.
  2. TrackLog every set as you go: reps, weight, notes, a form video.
  3. ProgressWilo reads it all and sets up the next one: heavier, lighter or different, for a reason.

Plan

A coach that knows your training.

Chat like you'd talk to a trainer. The coach reads your history and your notes before it writes a set.

  • Plans built from what you actually lifted, not a template
  • Remembers injuries, preferences and the exercises you hate
  • Workouts land on your days, ready on your phone

Mid-workout

Plans change. Your log doesn't break.

Machine taken? Shoulder tweaky? Tell the coach between sets. The new plan merges into the workout on your screen, live. Every set you've logged stays put.

  • Swap, add or drop exercises mid-session
  • Logged sets, notes and renames are never overwritten
  • Saves as you go, so nothing is lost if your phone dies
