I built Wilo to help me keep track of my training 
I’m a 52 year old powerlifter and veteran. I’m beat to shit. 
I found most workout trackers I tried created a lot of additional drag bc my training had a lot of rehab and sometimes things didn’t go according to plan bc of a lot of reasons. So then I’d get discouraged and just abandon the workout. 
So I built Wilo to help me change things up on the fly if I needed to and provide some guard rails to help me find something I could work on

Over time I found it really helped improve my consistency and show my micro wins despite having to
Change things up on the fly. 
So I wanted to share it with other people that love training

Also I had just gotten fired from one of the best workout trackers on the App Store for basically not being smart enough. I feel like the fact that now was 20 years older than everyone else there didn’t help and my dog Willow whom the app is named after peed on the floor
The first day. But anyways my pride and ego were hurt so I though I’d go study ml and data for a while
And then find a project where I can show my abilities. I really enjoy working on Wilo. It’s fun to apply the karpathy series and geek out on transformers but also build
Something that solves a real world problem

Everying I’ve done has been product driven. I made the tracker bc I got sick of reading workouts on text from Claude. And the features are designed to facilitate minimum cognitive load and workout completion. I built a inference model that is trained to be less chatting and focus on the simple tasks instead of being a Swiss army
Knife. So to apply small language models bc I think that’s better than tokenmaxing. 
And while you can build anything you want these days. Infra decisions still
Matter and product is still hard.  So I guess I wanted to surface my experience there to see if other people are interested.
